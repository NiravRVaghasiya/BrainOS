# ❌ Default Mode Network — Failures & Disorders

## Overactive DMN

### Depression (Rumination)
- **Problem**: DMN hyperconnected, especially mPFC-PCC loop
- **Result**: Persistent, uncontrollable negative self-referential thinking
- **Mechanism**: Can't switch from DMN → TPN (stuck in internal mode)
- **Experience**: "I keep replaying past failures and imagining future ones"
- **Memory impact**: Biased retrieval (only negative autobiographical memories accessible)
- **Evidence**: Depressed patients show 2-3x normal DMN-mPFC coupling

### Anxiety (Catastrophizing)
- **Problem**: DMN future simulation locked in threat-prediction mode
- **Result**: Vivid, repeated simulation of worst-case scenarios
- **Mechanism**: Amygdala hijacks DMN → all future simulations are threat-colored
- **Experience**: "I can't stop imagining everything that could go wrong"
- **Memory impact**: Creates false "memories" of disasters (imagined feels remembered)

### ADHD — DMN/TPN Switching Failure
- **Problem**: DMN intrudes during focused tasks (TPN can't suppress it)
- **Result**: Mind-wandering during work, losing track mid-sentence, daydreaming in class
- **Mechanism**: Weak anti-correlation between DMN and TPN (they co-activate)
- **Evidence**: ADHD brains show DMN activity DURING tasks that should suppress it
- **Stimulant medication**: Partially restores proper DMN suppression during focus

### OCD (Obsessive Loops)
- **Problem**: DMN generates intrusive thoughts that can't be dismissed
- **Result**: Repetitive, unwanted scenarios play on loop (contamination, harm, symmetry)
- **Mechanism**: Anterior cingulate (error monitor) + hyperactive DMN = "something's wrong" + simulation
- **Memory link**: Each obsessive simulation STRENGTHENS the associated fear memory

## Underactive DMN

### Autism Spectrum (Reduced Theory of Mind)
- **Problem**: DMN underconnected, particularly mPFC (social simulation) component
- **Result**: Difficulty predicting others' mental states, reduced spontaneous social simulation
- **Mechanism**: Less automatic mind-wandering about social scenarios
- **Memory impact**: Reduced autobiographical memory richness, poor episodic future thinking
- **Compensatory**: May develop explicit "social rules" (declarative) instead of intuitive (DMN)

### Depersonalization/Derealization
- **Problem**: Disconnection between DMN self-processing and incoming experience
- **Result**: "I feel like I'm watching my life from outside" / "Nothing feels real"
- **Mechanism**: mPFC hypoactivation → experiences not tagged as "mine"
- **Memory impact**: Memories form but don't feel personally relevant (alien autobiography)

## Neurodegenerative DMN Failure

### Alzheimer's Disease — DMN as Ground Zero
- **Critical finding**: Amyloid plaques accumulate FIRST in DMN regions
- **Why**: DMN regions are the most metabolically active → most vulnerable to metabolic stress
- **Sequence**: PCC → mPFC → Angular Gyrus → spread outward
- **Early sign**: Reduced DMN connectivity years before symptoms appear (potential biomarker)
- **Memory impact**: Autobiographical memory construction fails (can't access past self)

### Frontotemporal Dementia
- **Behavioral variant**: mPFC degradation → loss of self-awareness, social conduct
- **Result**: Personality changes, inappropriate behavior, loss of empathy
- **DMN impact**: Can't simulate others' minds (theory of mind collapses)
- **Memory**: Semantic self-knowledge degrades ("I am..." statements become impoverished)

## DMN in Altered States

| State | DMN Activity | Memory Effect |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Meditation (focused) | Decreased | Enhanced present-moment encoding |
| Meditation (open monitoring) | Altered (less self-referential) | Metacognitive clarity |
| Psychedelics (psilocybin) | DISRUPTED (entropy increased) | Ego dissolution, novel associations |
| Anesthesia | Completely suppressed | Amnesia (no self-process = no encoding) |
| Lucid dreaming | Partially activated | Awareness within dream narrative |
| Flow states | Suppressed mPFC, active creativity nodes | Enhanced procedural, reduced self-monitoring |

## The Clinical Paradox
- **Too much DMN**: Depression, anxiety, OCD, rumination
- **Too little DMN**: Loss of self, reduced creativity, impoverished inner life
- **Optimal**: Flexible switching between DMN (internal) and TPN (external) based on demands
- **Best predictor of mental health**: NOT DMN level, but FLEXIBILITY of DMN-TPN toggle
