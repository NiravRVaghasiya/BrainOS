# 🌙 Consolidation Queue (Sleep Replay)

## The Process
1. **Awake**: Experience encoded as hippocampal activity pattern
2. **NREM Sleep (Slow-Wave)**: Hippocampus REPLAYS the day's patterns (20x speed)
3. **Cortical Reception**: Neocortex receives replays and strengthens synaptic connections
4. **REM Sleep**: Integrates with existing knowledge, strips emotional charge, finds associations

## Evidence
- Rats navigating a maze: Same hippocampal firing patterns replay during sleep
- Students who sleep after studying outperform those who don't
- Targeted Memory Reactivation: Playing sounds/smells during sleep enhances associated memories

## How Long Does Consolidation Take?
- Hours: Initial stabilization
- Days–Weeks: Systems consolidation (hippocampus → cortex transfer)
- Months–Years: Full independence from hippocampus

## Napping Works
Even 20-min naps show consolidation benefits. The first NREM cycle (90 min) is most valuable.
