# 🔬 System Infrastructure — Real-World Examples

## Energy and Cognition

### 1. Decision Fatigue
- Judges grant 65% parole after breakfast, <10% before lunch (Danziger, 2011)
- After lunch: Back to 65%
- Glucose depletion → prefrontal cortex can't maintain effortful processing
- Default to easiest decision (deny parole = status quo)

### 2. Glucose and Exam Performance
- Students given glucose drink before exam outperform controls
- Effect size: ~0.5 standard deviations (meaningful)
- NOT about "more energy" — specifically fuels hippocampal/prefrontal processes
- Diminishing returns: More glucose ≠ indefinitely better (inverted U)

### 3. Hypoglycemia and Memory Blackouts
- Blood sugar drops severely → hippocampus stops encoding
- Diabetic patients during insulin reactions: Complete anterograde amnesia for the episode
- Proves: Hippocampal neurons are extremely glucose-sensitive

## Sleep's Role in Infrastructure

### 4. The All-Nighter Penalty
- 24h without sleep: Cognitive performance = legally drunk (BAC 0.10)
- Memory encoding drops 40% (hippocampus literally goes offline)
- Microsleeps occur: 1-30 second lapses where brain forces local sleep
- Two nights without sleep: Hallucinations begin (similar to psychosis)

### 5. Glymphatic Clearing Evidence
- Mice injected with fluorescent amyloid-beta:
  - Awake: Almost no clearing (waste accumulates)
  - Asleep: Rapid clearing (CSF flushes waste)
- Side sleeping > back sleeping for glymphatic flow (gravity assists drainage)
- Implications: Sleep position may matter for long-term brain health

### 6. Chronic Sleep Deprivation and Alzheimer's Risk
- Sleeping <6 hours/night in midlife → 30% higher Alzheimer's risk
- Mechanism: Amyloid-beta not cleared → plaques accumulate over decades
- Single night of sleep loss → measurable amyloid-beta increase in CSF
- Matthew Walker: "Sleep is the single most effective thing you can do to reset brain health"

## Myelination Examples

### 7. Why Teens Make Impulsive Decisions
- Prefrontal cortex not fully myelinated until ~age 25
- Limbic system (emotions, impulses) myelinates earlier → faster signals
- The "brakes" are slower than the "gas pedal" until mid-20s
- Not "bad decisions" — incomplete wiring (biological, not moral)

### 8. Musicians' White Matter
- Professional musicians have ~30% more white matter in corpus callosum
- More myelin = faster inter-hemispheric communication
- Develops WITH practice (not born with it) — plasticity of myelin
- Window: Most effective when started before age 7 (critical period for auditory myelination)

## Glial Cell Functions Visible in Life

### 9. "Brain Fog" After Infection (Neuroinflammation)
- COVID-19, flu, or any systemic inflammation
- Microglia become activated → release inflammatory cytokines in brain
- Result: Reduced synaptic efficiency, impaired attention, poor memory
- Resolution: Takes days-weeks for microglia to return to surveillance mode
- This is why you can't think clearly when sick — it's neuroinflammation, not "laziness"

### 10. Exercise and BDNF
- Aerobic exercise → muscles release BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)
- BDNF: Promotes new synapse formation, neurogenesis, LTP enhancement
- Hippocampus has highest BDNF receptor density (exercise literally grows memory hardware)
- 30 min moderate exercise: Measurable BDNF increase for ~2 hours
- Chronic exercise: Baseline BDNF stays elevated → sustained neuroplasticity boost
