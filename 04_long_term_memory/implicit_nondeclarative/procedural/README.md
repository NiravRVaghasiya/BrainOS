# 🚴 Procedural Memory — Skills & Habits

> "Knowing how" — expressed through action, not words.
> Brain region: Basal Ganglia + Cerebellum + Motor Cortex

## Examples:
- Riding a bicycle
- Typing on a keyboard
- Playing piano
- Driving a manual car
- Speaking your native language grammar

## Learning curve: Slow to form, extremely durable
- Requires many repetitions (deliberate practice)
- Once consolidated: nearly permanent (you don't "forget" how to ride a bike)
- Resistant to amnesia, aging, and interference

## The Automaticity Shift:
1. **Cognitive stage** — Slow, deliberate, requires full attention (learning to drive)
2. **Associative stage** — Fewer errors, less attention needed
3. **Autonomous stage** — Automatic, parallel processing, attention-free

## Why experts can't explain what they do:
Once procedural, knowledge is inaccessible to verbal/declarative systems.
The pianist "just plays" — asking them to explain disrupts performance.
